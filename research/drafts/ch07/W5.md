## Confirming a candidate splicing diagnosis

[NEU-298] @oquendo2024rnaseqcentric (2024) conf=high
  In the 86-patient blood study that started from the transcriptome rather than from a candidate variant, reverse-transcription PCR confirmed all 14 aberrant splicing events the sequencing had found, with six of the 14 already reported by an earlier publication, and independently found aberrant splicing in four further cases, in TERT, PRG4 and TAOK1, that the sequencing had not; of the 38 variants of uncertain significance with no detected event, 15 could not be assessed at all because the gene was too lowly expressed in blood.

[NEU-299] @decock2025pbmc (2025) conf=high
  A protocol built around short-term cultured peripheral blood mononuclear cells with and without cycloheximide, so that transcripts normally destroyed by nonsense-mediated decay survive to be seen, covered up to 80 per cent of an intellectual disability and epilepsy gene panel and found splicing defects in 6 of the 9 individuals in a 46-patient cohort who carried a splice variant; targeted cDNA analysis then confirmed aberrant splicing in four of those individuals but missed intron retention in two, and transcriptome-wide outlier calling for splicing, expression and monoallelic expression supported existing findings without producing a single new diagnosis.

## Diagnostic yield with its denominators

[NEU-283] @gonorazky2019boundaries (2019) conf=high
  Total RNA sequencing of muscle biopsies from 25 patients with monogenic neuromuscular disorders who were negative on exome sequencing, a gene panel or both resolved 36 per cent of them, 9 of 25 - genetic resolution being the only definition these authors give on the line read - with causative mutations in coding exons, non-coding exons and introns acting through transcriptional repression, exon skipping and intron inclusion.

[NEU-284] @fresard2019blood (2019) conf=high

[NEU-285] @yepez2026solverd (2026) conf=high

## Long reads in diagnosis, and where they do not help

[NEU-296] @hou2026longreaddiagnosis (2026) conf=high
  A long-read comparison independent of the one already in this chapter ran PacBio full-length sequencing and short reads on the same blood, fibroblast and lymphoblastoid samples from 50 individuals - 24 with an already known pathogenic RNA abnormality and 26 undiagnosed with a candidate DNA variant - and found that long reads added resolution or mechanism beyond short reads in 57 per cent of the control cases and 80 per cent of the discovery cases that had a positive RNA finding, while naming two limitations of their own: poor capture of short isoforms, with a median of 49 of 69 synthetic spike-in isoforms recovered against 67 of 69 by short reads, and uneven coverage within specific long transcripts.
  FIGURE: A matrix of the two long-read comparisons: rows for the failure modes each reports, columns for the two studies, so the reader sees which bound is replicated and which appears once.

[NEU-297] @hou2026longreaddiagnosis (2026) conf=high
  In that same 50-individual cohort one case turned on read length and on manual work: a neonate with a de novo 69-kilobase duplication of the ATAD3 gene cluster whose breakpoints DNA sequencing could not resolve because of homology across the cluster, where short-read RNA sequencing of fibroblasts was uninformative and gave no fusion-supporting reads. The long-read pipeline discarded the fusion transcript on internal polyadenylation priming and its gene fusion caller missed it as well; it was found only by reading the unfiltered alignments by hand against a purpose-built genome, and the transcript then proved to be a complete in-frame fusion of ATAD3A to ATAD3C whose exon-level breakpoints established the molecular diagnosis.

  Scoring 3,322 multi-exon monogenic disease genes for the sequencing depth each would need before splicing could be assessed - eight reads across 75 per cent of a gene's splice junctions, at the model's 95 per cent setting, within 100 million uniquely mapping reads - put 28.2 per cent of them within reach in whole blood, 49.4 per cent in lymphoblastoid cell lines, 43.6 per cent in skeletal muscle and 53.7 per cent in cultured fibroblasts, with 64.2 per cent reachable in at least one of the four.
  FIGURE: Four stacked bars, one per biosample, each split into reachable and unreachable fractions of the same 3,322-gene denominator, with the junction-coverage threshold written on the axis.

[NEU-264] @rowlands2022mrsd (2022) conf=high

## What a diagnostic transcriptome can actually call

[NEU-271] @yepez2022rnaseqclinical (2022) conf=high
  In the fibroblast workflow behind the 16 per cent clinical yield, the genes retained for testing covered 66 per cent of OMIM genes and 90 per cent of mitochondrial disease genes, but in simulations only half of injected halvings of expression - the effect size half the solved cases actually showed - were recovered unless the gene averaged about 450 reads, and at this cohort's median depth of 90 million reads only 55 per cent of OMIM genes averaged that much.

[NEU-274] @fresard2019blood (2019) conf=high
  A blood diagnostic pipeline built on 1,594 external control transcriptomes pooled from three cohorts plus 49 unaffected family members - with the expression-outlier enrichment itself computed against 899 of the external and 32 of the family controls - still left an average of 343 expression outlier genes per patient at an absolute Z score of 2 or more, which the eleven filtering steps together brought down to fewer than ten on average across all cases. The single filter restricting to under-expression outliers with phenotype matches and a nearby deleterious rare variant is reported separately, as cutting the candidate list to less than 1 per cent of the initial outliers while leaving 80 per cent of cases with at least one candidate gene.
  FIGURE: A funnel for a single patient: outlier genes at each filtering step, drawn for expression, splicing and allele-specific expression side by side on one log axis.

[NEU-275] @fresard2019blood (2019) conf=high
  Splicing outliers in the same blood cohort were more numerous still, an average of 540 outlier genes per sample at an absolute Z score of 2 or more, and restricting to phenotype-relevant genes with a deleterious rare variant within 20 bases of the outlier junction kept only 0.14 per cent of them, leaving at least one candidate in 32 per cent of cases.

[NEU-277] @mertes2021fraser (2021) conf=high
  For alternative donor usage, alternative acceptor usage and splicing efficiency, the median per-sample counts of genes with an aberrant splicing event were 12, 7 and 10 respectively, in a 119-sample fibroblast cohort, under a calling procedure that models each junction with a beta-binomial distribution, corrects within a splice site by Holm's family-wise method and then controls the false discovery rate genome-wide per sample, at a 10 per cent false discovery rate and an effect size above 0.3.

[NEU-278] @mertes2021fraser (2021) conf=high
  Two outlier-calling approaches were compared on GTEx data: replacing this method at a false-discovery rate of 0.1 with principal-component analysis at an absolute Z score of 2 produced two orders of magnitude more outlier calls across all GTEx tissues, and on artificially injected outliers in suprapubic skin dropped precision from 92 to 3 per cent while buying a recall increase from 83 to 98 per cent.
  FIGURE: A precision-recall curve with the two operating points marked - false-discovery-rate 0.1 and absolute Z score 2 - and the resulting per-sample call count written beside each.

[NEU-279] @mertes2021fraser (2021) conf=high
  Outlier calls that appear in only one tissue of an individual are common for every method tested: 22 per cent of the calls this method made at a nominal p of 10 to the minus 7 reappeared in another tissue of the same person at 10 to the minus 3, against 11 per cent for a competing method, and tissue-specific calls were less enriched for rare splice-affecting variants than replicated ones, from which those authors infer that more of the single-tissue calls are likely to be wrong.

[NEU-280] @scheller2023jaccard (2023) conf=high
  Replacing three partly redundant splicing metrics with a single intron-excision index and retuning the filters cut the number of splicing outliers called on 16,213 GTEx samples by roughly tenfold while raising the proportion of calls backed by a candidate rare splice-disrupting variant tenfold, and on 303 rare disease samples the same change lost only 2 of 22 previously identified pathogenic splicing cases.

[NEU-281] @yepez2022rnaseqclinical (2022) conf=high
  Run as a clinical workflow on 303 fibroblast samples, the three outlier tests together put the count of aberrant genes at a median of 25 per sample, with 8 of those having a known Mendelian disease association: a median of two under-expression and one over-expression outlier at a 5 per cent false discovery rate, and a median of 18 genes carrying at least one aberrantly spliced junction of which 6 were disease genes.

[NEU-294] @kremer2017rnaseqdiagnosis (2017) conf=high
  Auditing one pipeline against 40 patients whose diagnosis was already known showed which variant classes that pipeline can and cannot see: aberrant splicing detected 7 of 8 cases with a causal splicing variant, monoallelic expression recovered 3 of 6 patients with a missense variant compound with a stop or frameshift, aberrant expression recovered 3 of 4 homozygous stop variants, only 1 of 9 frameshift variants produced any detectable RNA defect, and for none of the 14 genes where a missense variant was causal could an RNA defect be detected with that pipeline.
