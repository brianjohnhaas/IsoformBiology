## Isoform-specific functional difference between 3R and 4R tau

[NEU-105] @falcon2018pickfold (2018) conf=high
  The isoform restriction in Pick disease is a property of the seed and not only of what the cell expresses: the sarkosyl-insoluble fraction from the Pick disease brain used for cryo-electron microscopy seeded aggregation of recombinant full-length 0N3R tau but not of 0N4R tau, across three independent experiments with separate protein preparations, and the fold cannot accommodate the bulkier K294 and V300 of the second repeat in place of T263 and Q269 of the first.

[NEU-107] @zhang2020cbdfold (2020) conf=high
  The seeding asymmetry runs in both directions: full-length 4R human tau, expressed in SH-SY5Y cells, let the sarkosyl-insoluble fraction from the three corticobasal degeneration brains seed aggregation of soluble tau, while cells expressing 3R did not, the reverse of the Pick disease result, and the structural reason the authors suggest is that where the last residue of the second repeat sits cannot accommodate the last residue of the first repeat.

[NEU-115] @adams2010tauassembly (2010) conf=high
  Mixing the two tau isoforms suppresses aggregation that either alone does not: heparin-induced polymerisation in vitro was lower for combinations of 3R and 4R tau than for 4R preparations alone, adding even small amounts of 3R to a 4R assembly reaction significantly decreased 4R assembly, and the effect held whether or not the reaction was reducing and whether or not the N-terminal inserts encoded by exons 2 and 3 were present.

[NEU-116] @microtubuledynamics2004taulivingcells (2004) conf=high  [ABSTRACT ONLY]
  The two tau isoforms stabilise microtubules to measurably different degrees inside cells: physiological amounts microinjected into cultured MCF7 cells expressing EGFP-tubulin suppressed microtubule dynamics for both isoforms, yet 3R tau's potency in stabilising most dynamic parameters was about threefold below that of 4R tau, and it barely suppressed shortening events at all.

[NEU-117] @dynamicinstability2005tausubpopulations (2005) conf=high  [ABSTRACT ONLY]

## TDP-43 autoregulation and the loss-of-function model

[NEU-176] @koyama2016tardbpauto (2016) conf=high

## Why the published fractions do not converge

[NEU-247] @sharo2023clinvarhgmd (2023) conf=high
  The two databases behind the published fractions do not classify to the same standard. [NEU-248] @sharo2023clinvarhgmd (2023) conf=high
  The reason the two databases are not comparable is structural. ## developmental regulation of the SCN1A poison exon

[NEU-347] @voskobiynyk2021poisonexon (2021) conf=high
  The exon looks like a developmental dimmer switch rather than an error: in a public RNA-seq series of mouse cortex, about 70 per cent of Scn1a transcripts carried exon 20N at embryonic day 14.5, falling gradually to under 10 per cent by postnatal day 30 and staying minimal thereafter, with total Scn1a mRNA rising as inclusion fell, and the homologous Scn8a poison exon 18N behaved the same way.

## dose-dependent protein rise in wild-type mouse brain

[NEU-345] @lim2020tango (2020) conf=high
  The same effect was produced in a live brain and it was titratable: a single intracerebroventricular injection of either oligonucleotide into wild-type neonatal mice at four doses from 0.3 to 20 micrograms gave, five days later, a dose-dependent fall in non-productive SCN1A mRNA, a dose-dependent rise in productive mRNA and a dose-dependent rise in NaV1.1 protein, with a non-targeting control at the top dose changing nothing.

## further poison exons in SCN1A as additional drug targets

[NEU-372] @tang2025scn1apoisonexons (2025) conf=high
  SCN1A turns out to carry three poison exons, not one: targeted deep-coverage long-read sequencing of SCN1A transcripts in neurons derived from healthy control stem cells found rare but consistent inclusion of two further frame-disrupting exons, one in intron 1 and one in intron 22, each flanked by canonical GT and AG splice sites and each introducing a premature termination codon, and their conservation differs sharply from the known exon, with 20N well conserved in mammals, 22N conserved in only some mammals and 1N not conserved outside primates, lying inside a short interspersed nuclear element.
